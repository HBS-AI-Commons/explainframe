# Direction: more revenue, less contribution

Learning question: why can contribution fall when revenue rises? Synthetic same-period dollars; contribution excludes fixed costs, cash timing and real cost drivers.

Persistent diagram: the whole bar is revenue; navy is variable costs; green is contribution. Labels accompany color. All states and the faint baseline use the same scale, fixed to $150. The left edge stays fixed. This is an arithmetic relationship, not evidence of a causal business mechanism.
