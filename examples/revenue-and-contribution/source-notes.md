# Original synthetic contribution example

## Worked example

All amounts describe the same illustrative period, in dollars. Before: revenue 100, variable costs 60, contribution 40. After: revenue 150, variable costs 120, contribution 30. Contribution is revenue less variable costs. Fixed costs and cash timing are excluded; the example does not establish profit or real-world cost drivers.

## Changed case

With revenue 150 and variable costs 105, contribution is 45. With revenue 150 and variable costs 110, contribution is 40, equal to the original. These are authored assumptions and arithmetic, not company results.
